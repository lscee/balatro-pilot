# Balatro 1.0.1o build metagame guide

This guide is a strategic prior for the installed Balatro 1.0.1o build. It is not a rigid tier list. Exact live card text, deck/stake rules, editions, stickers, Boss effects, and the current run state are authoritative. Judge packages by whether their actual effects solve the run's Chips, +Mult, XMult, consistency, and economy needs.

## What a winning build needs

- A durable Ante-8 build normally needs reliable Chips or strong hand levels, a source of additive Mult, multiplicative scaling, and enough consistency to trigger them. Economy and deck shaping buy time to assemble those layers.
- A famous strong Joker is not automatically a buy. It must have an open/replaceable slot, enough present support, affordable setup, and a plausible path before Blind scaling overtakes the run.
- Flexible engines and copy effects such as Blueprint/Brainstorm can justify a pivot because they reuse the best owned trigger. High-ceiling packages include Photograph plus Hanging Chad/face retriggers, Baron plus Mime with Kings/Steel/Red Seals and hand size, Hack/Fibonacci/rank payoffs with matching ranks and retriggers, suit/Flush payoffs backed by real suit density, and repeated-rank Four/Five-of-a-Kind plans backed by deck duplication/removal. Treat these as candidates, never as prerequisites.
- Pair/High Card and other low-card-count plans can be strong when repeatability, Planet levels, scaling Jokers, held-card effects, or Stuntman-style Chips compensate for low base score. Flush is only a durable plan when suit consistency and scoring/scaling support exist; an unsupported nominally stronger poker hand is not a build.
- By the late Antes, flat early-game bonuses without scaling or synergy are usually bridge pieces. Prefer a coherent Chips/+Mult/XMult engine over five individually decent Jokers.
- For collection-unlock runs, clearing Ante 8 is the terminal objective. Favor reliable multiplicative layers, copy effects, retriggers, deck concentration, and hand levels only insofar as they improve the probability of that clear; after the native victory checkpoint, return to the menu for the next legal deck/Stake target instead of entering Endless.

## Phase and economy policy

- Early game is normally Antes 1-2. Buy the minimum strength needed to clear upcoming Blinds reliably, then build interest and flexibility. A cheap scoring bridge is correct when survival is doubtful; it is not mandatory merely because a Joker slot is empty.
- If the current repeatable line already covers the next Blind with a safety margin, an offered Joker with only small flat value, no useful edition, no economy, no scaling, and no current/future synergy may be skipped. Preserve its price, the lost interest band, and the slot for a better offer.
- Default interest is earned in $5 bands, normally capped at $25. State the practical reserve and the spend trigger explicitly. Do not protect $25 when doing so risks death or passes a decisive durable upgrade; do not break an interest band for a weak placeholder whose expected value is below the lost money and slot flexibility.
- Reroll only with a purpose: a defined missing role, enough cash for both reroll and likely purchase, and adequate survival. Blind rerolling before the economy is established destroys future option value.
- Mid game is normally Antes 3-5. Convert accumulated money into repeatable scoring and scaling before targets outrun the build. Economy is a means to buy a winning engine, not a score to maximize.
- Late game is normally Ante 6 onward. Prioritize immediate reliability, XMult/retriggers, Boss resilience, and replacements for weak bridge pieces; speculative setup has less time to pay back.

## Pivot standard

- At every shop, compare three lines: keep the current plan, buy as a temporary bridge, or pivot the build. Evaluate the next Blind first, then Ante-8 ceiling.
- A mid-game pivot is justified when a newly offered high-impact Joker/package has materially higher ceiling or consistency, cash is sufficient after purchase, an owned weak/non-core slot can be sold or remains free, and the deck/Planets/consumables can support the new route in time.
- Prefer pivots with immediate standalone value plus attainable synergy, or with two or more supporting pieces already owned. Do not abandon a functioning invested build for one speculative combo piece that has no present trigger.
- Sunk cost is not a reason to keep a weak route. Preserve only investments that still increase future win probability. Reuse compatible pieces during a pivot and name which Joker, hand level, or deck edits become obsolete.
- `runPlan` must expose the current meta judgment, cash reserve/spend rule, and concrete pivot trigger. When a shop changes one of them, revise the plan immediately and explain why.
