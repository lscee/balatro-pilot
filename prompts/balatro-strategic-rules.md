# Balatro strategic rule capsule

This capsule is for strategic checkpoints. The exact game state, active card and Blind effects, locally generated legal candidates, and controller validation are authoritative. Never invent cards, effects, indices, money, slots, unlocks, or actions.

## Win the run, not one hand

- Judge survival against the remaining Blind target, Hands, Discards, exact remaining deck, hand levels, owned Jokers, consumables, active deck, and Boss effect.
- Preserve a repeatable scoring route. Use play/discard choices to improve that route; temporary survival deviations must be explicit.
- A non-scoring filler is useful only when it cycles a low-value card without breaking a hand, Joker condition, held-card effect, or future out.
- Treat local candidate classification, legal indices, out counts, and conservative score as authoritative lower-bound evidence.
- If a suit-changing Tarot or another owned consumable creates a locally projected hand that clears the remaining Blind, use it before a weaker play even when multiple Hands remain. Glass-card xMult is optimistic upside, not survival-safe guaranteed score.

## Build and economy

- Build only from owned or actually appeared support. Locked cards are impossible; unlocked unseen cards are optional future pivots, never current assets.
- Compare keeping the build, taking a temporary bridge, and pivoting. Pivot only when exact offered support, cash, slots, deck shape, hand levels, and remaining setup time make the new route stronger and reachable.
- Early weak Jokers may be skipped when survival is already reliable. Preserve interest and slot flexibility, but spend whenever survival, scaling, or a high-impact synergy is worth more than the reserve.
- At each shop rank every exact offer by immediate survival, repeatable scoring, scaling, consistency, economy, and opportunity cost. Reroll only with a defined target class and affordable downside.
- Consume every free reroll before leaving. Keep the last Joker slot open while underbuilt; by Ante 4 seek a dependable Chips source, additive Mult, and scaling/XMult unless an exact alternative package already clears future targets with margin.
- Bind item identity to its exact RPC area and zero-based index. The written purchase name and selected `card`/`voucher`/`pack` must be the same visible offer.

## Persistent plan

- Maintain one run plan linking active deck, owned build, target poker hand, shop priorities, economy reserve, pivot conditions, and next Blind/Boss milestone.
- Revise it only when purchases, sales, deck changes, hand levels, exact offers, Boss pressure, or survival evidence materially change the route.
- Exact visible effects override metagame priors. The installed-version guide is a prior, not permission to force a tier-list build.
